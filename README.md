# Jumpscare TV

An Android TV app. Press a button and a jumpscare appears with a loud scream.

- **PRESS** shows the jumpscare right away.
- **SURPRISE ME LATER** waits a random 5 to 20 seconds, then shows it.
- Press **Back** to skip a jumpscare or cancel the wait.

The face and the sound are made in code, so the app has no image or audio files.

## Warning
The app shows a sudden image and plays a loud sound. Only use it on people who are
fine with that, and keep the volume at a safe level. Do not use it on young children
or people with heart conditions.

## Install on an Android TV
1. Open the **Releases** page of this repo and copy the link to `Jumpscare-TV.apk`.
2. On the TV, install the free **Downloader** app, open the link in it, and install the APK.
   (Allow "Install unknown apps" for Downloader when the TV asks.)
3. Open **Jumpscare TV** from the apps row.

Every change pushed to `main` builds a new APK automatically (see the Actions tab).
